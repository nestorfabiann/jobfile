What changes
  • Early nudges on the project and as alerts when something is stuck: a change order waiting several days, no reply, a payment that does not match. Sent to the side holding things up, in friendly words.
  • “Report a problem” on every project, open to either side. Pick a type, describe it, add photos, say what would feel fair. The other side has 3 days to answer.
  • Answers are fixes, not blame: redo the work by a date, a credit, pay the balance, end the job, or “I see it differently” with an explanation. Accepting a fix records it on the project (a change order or a schedule item).
  • One shared summary built from the record: agreement, change orders, photos, payments and the timeline. Both sides see the same page.
  • Optional AI suggestions of two or three middle-ground options. They only suggest. Either side can ignore them.
  • Mediator queue (admin only): problems stuck past the deadline land with the full record. You send a non-binding recommendation to both sides.
  • Nothing is binding unless both accept, and Jobfile never holds money. If they still disagree, they see outside options (community dispute resolution, consumer complaint channels). Wording to be reviewed by a lawyer before it goes live.
  • Not in this phase: scanning private messages, automatic nudges based on message tone, paid mediation.
