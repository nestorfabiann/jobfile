Which one fits?

  • Lead and partner. One contract. Best when the trades overlap, like painting and drywall, and one company coordinates and stands behind the whole job. The lead pays the partner and the homeowner reviews the lead.
  • Separate contracts. Two contracts. Best for unrelated trades, like painting and electrical, or licensed trades. The homeowner pays each company directly and reviews each one.

Same in both

  • Local only. Partners are verified companies within 50 miles of the lead and in the project's area.
  • Partner confirms first. Nothing reaches the homeowner with a price the partner has not agreed to.
  • Payments are records only. Jobfile does not move money.

Trade-off with separate contracts

  • No single owner of the whole job. If one company drops out after hiring, the other's job still goes on, and the homeowner decides what to do. The bundle can only be withdrawn before the homeowner hires.
  • The homeowner coordinates. The schedule note helps, but they manage two companies.
