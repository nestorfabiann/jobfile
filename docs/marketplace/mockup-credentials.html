What changes
  • New Credentials section in the business profile: BBB, chamber or local association, trade group, manufacturer, EPA lead-safe, license, or other.
  • A link is the best proof. A link to their own BBB or association page lets homeowners click and check. Uploading a certificate also works.
  • Two labels. “Stated by contractor” shows right away. “Checked by Jobfile” appears after an admin approves the proof or listing, the same way insurance works.
  • No BBB or association logos. Names as text with a link, because many groups restrict logo use.
  • Shows in three places: the public profile, the bid list next to each bidder (top two or three), and the profile preview and completeness checklist as an optional item.
  • You decide how much to review. Admin gets a short queue of items waiting for a check. Anything not checked stays labeled “Stated”.
  • Reports. Homeowners and other contractors can report a credential that looks false, and you can remove it.
