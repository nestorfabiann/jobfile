How it works
  • When it appears. When the homeowner taps "The work is finished", or when the contractor starts closeout.
  • Five confirmations. Scope lines, extra work, payments, punch list and photos, and an optional note. Scope lines come straight from the agreement.
  • Both sides confirm their own side. Each sees what the other said.
  • Payments. The contractor can record payments that happened outside the app. The homeowner can say "I paid more". A difference shows to both sides and needs a yes or no from the contractor.
  • Never a lock. A disagreement can be saved with a note and the job can still be closed. Nobody can hold the job hostage.
  • It feeds My money. Payments confirmed here become the recorded payments in the contractor's totals.
  • It goes in the project record PDF, with who confirmed what and when.
  • Records only. Jobfile does not hold or move money.
