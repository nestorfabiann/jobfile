What changes
  • Number next to Community. A red count in the menu for unread replies to your posts and approval notices on posts you shared (screen 1).
  • Dot on the Menu tab. The bottom bar shows a small red dot while the count is above zero, so you know to open the menu.
  • Clears when you look. Opening a post, or the reply from My posts (screen 3), marks it read.
  • “New” tag stays. It still means fresh posts from others since your last visit (screen 2). The number and the tag never show together; the number wins.
  • Out of Projects. Community alerts leave the red Projects badge and Needs you. Phone alerts and the Recent alerts list do not change.
  • Both roles. Homeowners and contractors get the same count.
  • Not built yet. Mockup only; the numbers are examples.
