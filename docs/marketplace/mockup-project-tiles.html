What changes
  • Two tiles side by side instead of two stacked buttons. Orange filled tile for the main action, white outlined tile for the other, each with a small icon and a one-line hint.
  • The long paragraph goes away. "Hired someone on your own?" moves into the "?" help for this page and the first screen after you tap the tile.
  • Saves about a third of the space above your projects, so the first project card shows without scrolling.
  • First-time homeowners: the tile says "Post your first project" until they have one.
  • Contractors get the same pair: "Start a project" and "Find work" (today "Find work" is a lone button).
  • Same destinations as today. Nothing else about the pages changes.
