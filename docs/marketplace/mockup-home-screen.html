Add to Home Screen

1Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow pointing up).
2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
3Tap Add. Jobfile appears with your other apps.
iPhone only allows this from Safari, so if they opened the link in another app, the screen says "Open this page in Safari first" with a Copy link button. Photos, alerts and sign-in all work the same inside the Home Screen version.
