Quick answers

Shown above the form on the Help page, so many people find the answer first.
Is Jobfile really free?

Yes. Free to join, no subscription, no fees at all.

How do I post a job?

Tap Post, pick the kind of job, tap what you want done, and add photos. Local contractors send bids.

Who can see my address?

Only the contractor you hire. Contractors who bid see your ZIP code and first name.

How do payments work?

Jobfile records milestone payments. You pay your contractor directly, and both sides confirm it in the record.

A contractor asked for my phone number

You do not have to share it before you hire someone. Keep chatting in Jobfile, and report anything that feels off.

How do I change or delete my account?

Menu, then Settings. Deleting removes your account and your posts.
