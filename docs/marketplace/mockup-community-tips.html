Pro tipPainting

Prime stained walls twice, not once

Water and smoke stains bleed through one coat of paint. Use a stain-blocking primer, let it dry fully, then prime again before you paint.

Pro tipElectrical

A breaker that keeps tripping is a warning

Do not just reset it again and again. It can mean an overloaded circuit or a fault.

Electrical work is for licensed pros. This is general advice only.

What changes
  • A Tips filter beside All, Projects and Ideas in Community. Tips can be filtered by trade like the others.
  • Short tip cards. Title, trade, 2 to 5 lines (up to about 400 characters) and an optional photo.
  • Pro tip or Homeowner tip tag. It shows who wrote it, so readers can weigh it.
  • Helpful button replaces the heart on tips. The most helpful sort first.
  • Same safety rules. No phone numbers, emails or links. Reply and Report work as they do now. Jobfile reviews tips before they go live.
  • Safety note on risky trades. Electrical, gas, structural and roofing tips get a line saying to use a licensed pro.
  • Starter tips. I would write 5 to 8 for you to approve so the tab is not empty on day one.
  • Share a tip becomes one of the choices when someone taps Share a project or Ask for ideas. Exact placement is for you to decide.
