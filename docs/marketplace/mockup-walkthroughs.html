Where they would show up
  • Landing page: "How Jobfile works" under the hero, 6 steps, about 20 seconds.
  • Help sheet (the ? button): a short animation above the written answer for topics like Bid as a team and Meet before you hire.
  • First visit to a screen: a dismissible "Watch how it works" card.
  • Contractor onboarding: profile, insurance and ID check.
  • Closeout and problems: how a problem is reported and resolved.

Keep it to 3 or 4 well-placed ones, not one on every screen.

What changes
  • Built into the page, not video files: tiny, fast, easy to update when a process changes.
  • Each step has a caption, a progress bar you can tap, Pause and Replay.
  • Reduced motion: no autoplay, steps shown one by one with a Play button.
  • Under 20 seconds, 4 to 6 steps, one idea each.
  • Text version always available in the Help sheet.
  • No database step. Not built yet.
