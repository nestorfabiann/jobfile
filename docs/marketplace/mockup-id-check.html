What changes
  • Optional "Verify your identity" step in the business profile. Photo of a driver license or state ID, an optional selfie, and the name on it.
  • Homeowners only see a badge: “ID verified by Jobfile” with the month, on the profile and in the bid list. Never the ID, number, birth date or address.
  • Private and short-lived. The photo goes to private storage that only admins can open. Each open is written to the audit log. Approving or rejecting deletes the photo and selfie, so Jobfile keeps no ID images.
  • What you check: the name matches the business owner, the ID is not expired, and the photo looks genuine. A short checklist on the admin screen.
  • Careful wording. “ID verified” confirms a real person, not a license or insurance, and the badge says so.
  • Ready to switch later. If volume grows, we can swap the manual check for a service like Stripe Identity without changing what homeowners see.
  • Not required for now. It is an extra badge, separate from “Verified by Jobfile”.
