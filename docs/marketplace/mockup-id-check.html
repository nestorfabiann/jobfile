What changes
  • Optional "Verify your identity" step in the business profile. The contractor uploads one photo of a driver license or state ID.
  • We read it automatically. An AI service reads the name and expiry date, checks them against the owner name on the account, and shows the contractor what it read so they can fix mistakes. The upload screen says plainly that an AI service processes the photo.
  • Homeowners only see a badge: “ID verified by Jobfile” with the month, on the profile and in the bid list. Never the ID, number, birth date or address.
  • Private and short-lived. The photo goes to private storage that only admins can open. Each open is written to the audit log. Approving or rejecting deletes the photo, so Jobfile keeps no ID images.
  • Pre-filled review for you. Your screen shows what was read, whether it matches, and a green "Looks good" or an orange "Needs a look" with the reasons (close but not exact name, blurry, looks like a photo of a screen). You approve or reject in one tap.
  • No auto-approval. A fake ID can read perfectly, so a person always decides. If we later move to a service that checks documents for tampering, that step can become automatic.
  • Careful wording. “ID verified” confirms a real person, not a license or insurance, and the badge says so.
  • Ready to switch later. If volume grows, we can swap the manual check for a service like Stripe Identity without changing what homeowners see.
  • Not required for now. It is an extra badge, separate from “Verified by Jobfile”.
