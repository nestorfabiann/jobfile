How it behaves

  • Once only. Saved on the account and the phone, so it never comes back, even after signing out.
  • Not forced. "Not now" is always there. Nothing blocks reaching Projects.
  • Skipped if alerts are already on, the browser can't do alerts, or they dismissed the card before.
  • Installed later? If someone chose "Not now" in Safari, the installed app shows the Turn on screen once, since that is the first time it can work.
