Protecting a contractor's invited customer

What I found in the app today: a project a contractor opens for a homeowner has no link to anything the homeowner posts on the marketplace later. The first contractor is never told, and the project's quote and notes stay private to its members.

What changes

  • The invited project stays private. It never shows on Find work. (Already true. We keep it that way.)
  • If the homeowner posts the same kind of job within 60 days, the first contractor gets an alert and a place on the bid list.
  • A short note on the Post page tells the homeowner this will happen. Nothing is blocked.
  • When a homeowner closes the project, they can pick a reason and the contractor sees it.
