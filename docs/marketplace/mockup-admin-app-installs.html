How it works

  • Installed means the person opened Jobfile from its Home Screen icon at least once. It is noted on each visit.
  • Alerts on comes from the phones that turned alerts on. On iPhone that only works from the installed app.
  • Starts at launch. Anyone who installed before this goes live shows as installed after their next visit.
  • Removing the icon is not detectable, so "installed" means "has used it that way", not "still on the Home Screen".
  • Admins only. Same access as the other Usage pages.
