What changes
  • Three tabs under the top tiles: Ongoing, Bids and Done, each with a count. One group shows at a time, so nothing is far down the page.
  • Opens on what matters. Ongoing if you have hired projects, otherwise Bids. After you post a project it opens on Bids.
  • Bids shows a red number when new bids came in that you haven’t looked at.
  • Done replaces the closed “Completed” box. Same projects, one tap away, with a Rate it nudge where a rating is missing.
  • Everything else stays: the two tiles, the “You’re all caught up” bar and the project cards look the same.
  • Contractors. Their Projects page has the same long scroll. I would give it matching tabs (Hired, Bids, Done) after you see this one.
