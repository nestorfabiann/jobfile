How it behaves

  • Separate from the Jobfile score. Never averaged in.
  • Shown as the source reports it, with its name and a link, refreshed about once a day.
  • Nothing shows until the name and city match, so no one can borrow a competitor's page.
  • Hidden if the link breaks or the business is removed.
  • Google first, then Yelp. Google needs an API key from your Google Cloud account.
