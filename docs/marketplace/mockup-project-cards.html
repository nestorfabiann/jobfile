What changes
  • Summary strip. Counts for new bids, open and hired projects. The green tile shows when something is waiting.
  • Action line on every card. New bids: "Compare bids". No bids: "Add photos" or "Invite a contractor". Closing soon: "Extend".
  • Price range bar. Shows the typical range at a glance.
  • Colored edge and icon tile. Same look as the draft cards and menu tiles, so cards are easy to tell apart.
  • Detail level bar. Shows how complete the post is, so homeowners know adding detail gets more bids.
  • Post a project. Keeps its place, with a "+" and a time estimate. It could shrink once there are several projects.
  • The "?" button. Sits above the bottom bar and no longer covers a card's bid count.
