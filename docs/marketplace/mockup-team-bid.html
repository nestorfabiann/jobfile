Contractors see "Bid as a team" under "Place a bid" on any open job. When the job lists more than one trade, as here, a note suggests teaming up. Tapping it opens screen 1 with this job filled in. Homeowners and the job's own poster never see the button.

Rules built in

  • One lead is accountable. The homeowner hires and rates the lead. The partner works for the lead.
  • Both companies must be verified to join a team bid, and insurance for each shows on the bid.
  • Partner confirms first. Nothing goes to the homeowner with a price the partner has not agreed to.
  • The homeowner's contact stays private until they hire, then only the lead has it by default.
  • Records only. Payments between the homeowner, lead and partner are logged, not moved.
  • Ratings: the homeowner rates the lead. The lead can rate their partner.
