Who can do what

  • Lead. Adds partners, sets their part, stays the contact, records payouts.
  • Partner. Sees only their scope, price, dates, photos and messages with the lead.
  • Homeowner. Is told, sees the partner's rating and insurance, can remove them.

Same as team bids

  • Local only. Within 50 miles of the project.
  • Partner confirms first. Nothing is final until they accept the price.
  • Payments are records only. Jobfile does not move money.
