What changes
  • List | Map toggle under the title on Projects near you. List is the default and works exactly as today.
  • Same filters. Trade and distance chips apply to both views.
  • Pins by trade. Each pin has the trade’s color and letter. A blue dot shows your business ZIP, and a dashed circle shows your service area.
  • Tap a pin and a small card slides up with the title, price range, bids and an Open project button. Pins that sit close together group into a numbered circle; tapping it lists those projects.
  • No exact addresses. Pins sit at the ZIP code’s center with a small random offset so jobs in one ZIP do not stack. The card shows the ZIP only.
  • Map service. Starts on free OpenStreetMap tiles, easy to swap for a provider with a nicer look later. No billing to set up now.
  • Contractors only. Homeowners do not see other homeowners’ projects, so this view is for contractors.
  • Sparse at first. With few posts there are few pins. A short line says “3 projects in your area” so it never feels broken.
