How it works
  • Profile. Three actions: Invite to bid (needs a project), Ask a question (general or about a project), Start a project (posts it and invites them).
  • Invite to bid stays project-based. A bid needs a scope, a size and a place. With no project, the button becomes "Start a project and invite them".
  • General questions. No project needed, clearly labeled, and never a bid request.
  • Same privacy. Phone numbers, emails and web links are blocked until a bid is accepted.
  • Caps. 3 new general conversations a day per homeowner, and one per contractor until they reply. Contractors within 50 miles only.
  • Contractor controls. A "Pass" button that doesn't count against them, and a one-tap nudge to ask the homeowner to post a project.
  • Links up later. When the homeowner posts a project, the thread can be attached to it so nothing starts over.
  • Hidden and unlisted companies can't be messaged.
