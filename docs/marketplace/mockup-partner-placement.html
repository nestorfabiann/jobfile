What changes
  • Stepper and "needs you" come first. Right under the project header, where people look first.
  • No partners yet: one compact row at the bottom. A dashed "+" circle, a one-line explanation and an Add button. The long list of what partners can and cannot see now shows on the Add screen, after the tap.
  • Partners added: a slim team strip at the top (colored initials and status), because it tells you who is on the job. The full list with scope, price and dates sits lower, with "Add another partner".
  • The Team tab is unchanged. It still has the full-size screen for adding and managing partners.
  • Homeowners see the team strip when partners exist, and nothing when there are none.
