The three new tabs are dashed. The tab row grows from 2 rows to 3. A red dot on a tab means something needs you, for example a selection due in 2 days. The contractor's "My money" tab stays where it is.

Why these three

Schedule: 41% of homeowners in a Houzz survey said a clear timeline would have helped.

Selections: late choices are a common cause of delay, and the agreement already says they can move the schedule.

Documents: permits, insurance, warranties and receipts in one place.
