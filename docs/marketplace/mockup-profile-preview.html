What changes
  • A mini card replaces the paragraph. Logo or initial, company name, rating, area, three work photos and trades, built from what is saved.
  • A completeness bar and short checklist. Done items show a check; missing ones say "Add" and open that part of the editor.
  • New contractors see dashed blanks ("+" photo slots, "Add your trades") so it is clear what homeowners will not see yet.
  • Two buttons stay. Edit profile and View public page, side by side.
  • Only things homeowners can see are previewed. Insurance shows as approved only after an admin approves the certificate.
  • No new data. Nothing is stored; the preview reads the same profile, photos and ratings as the public page.
