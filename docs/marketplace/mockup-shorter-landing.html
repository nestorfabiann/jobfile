What changes
  • Swipeable tiles. The four or five “What you get” tiles become one sideways row. One tile shows at a time with dots under it. Nothing is removed. This is the biggest saving in height.
  • Compact “already have a contractor” card. Headline, one line and the two buttons. The three steps open with a “How it works” tap.
  • Open near you. Shows one job card and the “See all open jobs” button. For contractors the section moves above the tiles, since live local jobs are the strongest reason for them to sign up.
  • Unchanged. Hero and role switch, the tappable three steps, the four trust icons, “And also” and the final call to action.
  • Approximate effect. I estimate the page ends up roughly a third shorter on a phone. I have not measured it.
  • Not built yet. Mockup only. Deploys are paused by Vercel’s daily limit, so this would go out with the next batch.
