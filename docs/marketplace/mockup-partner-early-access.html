What changes
  • Early access window. New projects show to partner-insured contractors first. The delay for everyone else is yours to set per partner (24 hours here).
  • Countdown on the card. Partner-insured contractors see “Early access” and the time left. Other contractors see a short note that new projects are coming, and nothing about which ones.
  • Confirmed by the partner. Early access turns on only for contractors on the insurer’s policyholder list. A certificate that names the partner but is not on the list goes to your review queue.
  • Open about it. The profile tag reads “Insured by Acme Mutual”, and Help explains that partners get early access.
  • One switch. Turn a partner on or off, change the window, or end it. When it ends, everything goes back to showing to everyone at once.
  • Homeowners are not affected. They still get bids from the whole pool, only slightly later from non-partner contractors.
  • Not built yet. This is a mockup of the idea. No partner is set up.
