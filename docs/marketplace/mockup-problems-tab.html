What changes
  • New tab: Problems. Opens the list of problems on this project, with the Report a problem form. Past problems and how they were settled stay there.
  • Wide tab (screen 1). The nine tabs keep their tidy 3 by 3 grid. Problems sits below as a full-width bar, so there is no lone button on its own row. It says “None open” when all is calm.
  • Tenth small tab (screen 2). The grid becomes 4 columns with the tenth tab at the end. Smaller buttons, and the last row is mostly empty.
  • Red dot. When a problem is open and waiting on you, the tab shows a red dot and a line like “1 open, waiting on you” (screen 3).
  • The Overview card stays. “Something not right?” remains as a second way in.
  • When it shows. The tab shows once the agreement is signed, same as the Overview card.
