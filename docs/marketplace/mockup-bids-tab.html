How it fits together

One tab, four statuses

1Waiting: you sent a bid, the homeowner hasn't chosen. You can edit or withdraw.
2Won: the homeowner accepted. A Jobfile project opens and the bid links to it.
3Not selected: someone else was chosen, or the job closed. Locked, kept for your records.
4Withdrawn: you took it back. Hidden from the list unless you filter for it.
You get alerts for
A bid accepted, a bid not selected, and a homeowner's question about your bid.
Needs the marketplace first
Bids only exist once homeowners can post jobs and contractors can bid on them. This tab is built together with that feature.
